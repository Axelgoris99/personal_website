Two years of intensive mathematics, physics and computer science (Python) to prepare for the competitive entrance exams of French engineering schools.

- Maths fundamentals: analysis, algebra, topology.
- Physics: mechanics, quantum mechanics, waves, thermodynamics.
- Training: 2 hours of oral exams and 4 hours of written exams every week.

It led me to [Centrale Nantes](/education/centrale).
